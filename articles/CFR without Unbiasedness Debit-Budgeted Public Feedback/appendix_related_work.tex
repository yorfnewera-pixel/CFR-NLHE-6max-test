\paragraph{Sampling-based CFR.}
MCCFR partitions terminal histories and constructs a sampled counterfactual
value whose expectation equals the dense value
\citep{lanctot2009mccfr}.  Public Chance Sampling specializes the partition to
public outcomes and exploits vectorized reuse over private chance
\citep{johanson2012pcs}.  Generalized sampling permits any bounded unbiased
value estimator and makes estimator variance explicit
\citep{gibson2012generalized}; stochastic regret minimization similarly
combines a regret minimizer with conditionally unbiased gradient estimates
\citep{farina2020stochastic}.  A deterministic remaining batch in a
without-replacement epoch has no such eventwise conditional law.  DB-CFR
instead compares the realized delivered sum directly with the dense target
sum and charges only target-regret underestimation.

\paragraph{Order and delayed computation.}
Random reshuffling, quasi--Monte Carlo integration, and GraB connect component
order or discrepancy with temporal variation
\citep{niederreiter1992qmc,lu2022grab,das2022withoutreplacement}.  Lazy CFR
uses reach structure to update a subset of information sets while aggregating
their skipped counterfactual rewards \citep{zhou2020lazy}.  These are important
ingredients and analogies.  The nonroutine step here is the full-game
composition: centered public components are evaluated at native alternating
origins, transferred through local counterfactual regret, and constrained by
a causal one-sided debit with an exact feasible action.

Online learning with delayed feedback instead postpones the loss, gradient,
or reward needed for the learner update
\citep{joulani2013delay,flaspohler2021delay}.  In DB-CFR, the selected partial
row reaches RM/RM+ immediately and changes the next policy; only the separate
dense-target audit is unsettled.  The outstanding correction therefore enters
the delivered-to-target transfer as a debit reserve rather than as delayed
learner regret.  This distinction also separates the theorem from generic
asynchronous CFR implementations, which change when regret updates are
applied.

\paragraph{Approximate feedback and execution.}
Functional and approximate regret matching charge per-round input error
\citep{waugh2015functional,dorazio2019approximate}.  Such a symmetric charge
is valid but can discard the signed cancellation within a balanced public
epoch.  Equation~\ref{eq:app-weighted-deadline-identity} retains this
cancellation.
Dataflow systems and parallel CFR supply grouping, progress, and physical
execution mechanisms \citep{akidau2015dataflow,murray2013naiad,kim2026parallel};
the event-faithful contract uses those ideas only to preserve one declared
learner transition.  They do not define the target-game row or its regret
transfer.

\paragraph{Concurrent persistent MCCFR.}
A concurrent anonymous submission studies a distinct failure mode:
alternating External-Sampling traversals adaptively thin a persistent
concrete-node chance stream into learner-specific subsequences
\citep{anonymous2027amccfr}.  Its owner--node key, sampled-tree recursion, and
opponent martingale do not appear here.  Conversely, DB-CFR's public width,
one-sided debit, complete-coverage fallback, HUNL frontier, and public-Leduc
controller do not appear in that work.  Both use finite summation by parts as
classical algebra.
